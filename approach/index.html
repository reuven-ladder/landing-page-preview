Questions

An experienced cost professional, named in your proposal with their experience and scope of review.

No. Describe the project first. We ask for documents once the scope is clear. NDA on request.

We price what’s drawn, carry stated allowances for the rest, and list what’s missing.
How is AI used?
It reads the documents and measures quantities. Cost professionals lead pricing and decide anything that needs judgment.
