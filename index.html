Questions

Before you ask for a quote.

More on how we work in Why Ladder.

Is this an AI estimate?
No. AI reads, counts and cross-checks the documents, which is the part no one has time to do thoroughly. Cost professionals price the work, review it and sign it off. Every quantity links back to the sheet or spec section it came from, so you never have to take an AI’s word for a number.
What do I receive?
A cost estimate or quantity takeoff in Excel, organized by trade, with the basis of every line: where the quantity came from, how it was priced and what is still open. Each package comes with marked-up drawings that show its evidence. See a sample estimate.
How is AI used?
Specialist AI agents, one for each trade, read every drawing, specification and addendum in their part of the project, take off quantities and flag conflicts. A review step checks each trade against what it usually carries, and neighboring trades are reconciled so scope doesn’t fall between them. Our cost engineers lead the work and own the estimate.
Who is responsible for the estimate?
Our cost engineers. They direct the analysis, review the pricing and sign off the deliverable. The cost professional on your project is named in your proposal, with their experience and scope of review.
Do you replace our estimators?
No. We take on the reading and takeoff load, so your estimators can spend their time on pricing, strategy and the bids they would otherwise have to pass on.
Can the AI get it wrong?
Yes, the same way a person can. That’s why every quantity says where it came from (printed, calculated, measured or judgment) and links back to the sheet or spec section. A cost professional reviews the items that move the number. We state what the analysis covered rather than promise it found everything.
What happens when the documents don’t answer a question?
It gets one of four treatments: a clarification for the design team, a stated assumption, an allowance or an exclusion. Each is listed with the estimate, so you can see what the number depends on.
How early can you start?
At concept. Early estimates matter most, because that’s when cost can still change the design. We work from whatever the project has, and the estimate gets more detailed as the design does.
Can you update the estimate when the design changes?
Yes. Scope, quantities and assumptions stay connected to their sources, so a revision is assessed against the existing cost basis instead of estimated from scratch.
How is it priced?
A fixed fee per scope of work. We confirm the scope, fee and delivery schedule with you before starting. You don’t need to send drawings to get a quote, and we sign an NDA on request.
