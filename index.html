How it works

From your documents to a reviewed estimate.

Every line traces back to a sheet or specification section, so your team can check a number, question an assumption or answer a clarification without redoing the estimate.

  1. 01 · Documents

    We start with what you already have.

    Drawings, specifications, addenda and revisions, read together as one set. AI does the document-intensive reading.

  2. 02 · Scope and quantities

    Every quantity points to its source.

    Areas, counts and lengths are measured and linked to the sheet or specification they came from. Missing information becomes a clarification, not a guess.

  3. 03 · Pricing and review

    Quantities become a reviewed estimate.

    Cost professionals apply rates, allowances and assumptions, and review the judgment calls before anything is delivered.

Three lines from the estimate, and the judgment behind them

A quantity linked to its drawing
TPO roofing, 60 mil, fully adhered18,400 SF × $23.90 = $439,760

Measured on A-501, specified in 07 54 23 §2.2. Rate from regional bids, range $410k – $470k.

An allowance with a stated basis
Selective demolition, interior partitions25,600 SF × $1.85 = $47,360

No existing-conditions survey yet (D-101, note 4). Carried as an allowance and flagged until the survey arrives.

A clarification that moves the price
Curtain wall, thermally broken6,900 SF · $0.96M – $1.22M

Head height at level 3 is not dimensioned on A-301. Priced as a range, with RFI #7 drafted for the design team.
